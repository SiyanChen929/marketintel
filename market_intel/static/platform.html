Allocation policy · 资金分配规则

每次决策只读取此前 36 个月。风险尺度取 max（日历收益标准差，活动期收益标准差，活动期最差损失 ÷ 3，0.30%）。日频策略至少 20 个活动日；事件策略至少 12 个事件日期。风险倒数分配资金，单策略上限 40%、事件策略上限 10%，剩余资金为现金。该规则未优化历史 Sharpe；活动期统计不年化。源策略仍使用各自研究逻辑。

Paper ledger

virtual PAPER/SHADOW sessions · virtual cash and virtual positions in an append-only ledger · no broker connection exists

Paper engine limits. Execution is intraday only: an order or a mark that crosses a UTC session date fails closed until trading-calendar, dividend, split, symbol-change and financing accounting are connected. Short selling and margin are refused. These are documented engine limits, not values read from the API.

Currency is fixed to USD: quote-currency and point-in-time FX conversion are not implemented, and the API refuses any other currency. Every session is submitted as USD.

The platform API refuses allow_short=true and allow_margin=true until borrow/locate, financing, collateral and dividend/corporate-action accounting are implemented. Every session is submitted with both set to false.
